forest_leop_may_2026_data_used.csv — column guide
================================================================

This file lists every recorded FOREST ground-station contact from the
forest_leop_may_2026.docx report (61 rows = all 61 recorded contacts;
15 fitted, 11 primary). One row per contact. Empty cells mean the value
does not apply to that contact (e.g. no fit, or no GPS data).

Units and conventions
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Times are UTC ISO-8601 strings.
- Position errors are 3-D Euclidean distances in kilometres (km).
- Corrections are fitted by a batch least-squares (soft-L1 / Gauss-Newton)
  Doppler fit; GPS is used ONLY to score error afterwards, never inside the fit.

IDENTITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
satellite         Which spacecraft: FOREST-16 / 17 / 18 / 19.
contact_id        UUID of one recorded ground-station contact.
station           Ground station that made the recording
                  (PUNTA_ARENAS, SVALSAT, TROLL, AWARUA, HARTEBEESTHOEK).

TLE (initial orbit estimate)
----------------------------------------------------------------
tle_role          Role of the TLE in this report:
                  "analysis_prior" = the spacecraft-specific initial orbit
                  estimate used for the fit; "recorded_only" = a TLE recorded
                  for a contact that was never fitted.
tle_epoch_utc     Epoch (reference time) of that TLE.
tle_line1         TLE line 1 (identification + mean motion, drag terms).
tle_line2         TLE line 2 (orbital elements: inclination, RAAN,
                  eccentricity, argument of perigee, mean anomaly, mean motion).

  NOTE: this report does NOT generate new TLEs. The estimator keeps the
  initial orbit estimate fixed and instead fits a time correction and a
  frequency correction per contact. These TLEs are the fixed priors.

TIME WINDOWS
----------------------------------------------------------------
recorded_start_utc / recorded_end_utc
                  Span of raw telemetry collected for this contact.
accepted_start_utc / accepted_end_utc
                  Span of the accepted Doppler measurements actually used.

DATAPOINT COUNTS (selection funnel, most inclusive -> least)
----------------------------------------------------------------
raw_telemetry_samples   Total telemetry samples recorded for the contact.
doppler_nonnull         Samples that had a carrier-frequency-offset value
                        present (not null).
doppler_nonzero         Samples whose frequency offset was non-zero.
doppler_above_floor     Samples with |offset| >= 0.1 Hz.
accepted_doppler_measurements
                        Samples that also passed the validity filters and were
                        used in the fit. Filters (from the report):
                          - frequency offset present and non-zero
                          - |offset| >= 0.1 Hz
                          - antenna elevation > 1 deg and < 89 deg
                        A contact had to have >= 301 of these to be fitted.

GPS
----------------------------------------------------------------
gps_fixes_in_window     Number of GPS position records inside the accepted
                        Doppler window. Used only to score error after the fit.

FIT STATUS
----------------------------------------------------------------
fitted            True if the contact had >= 301 accepted Doppler
                  measurements and the estimator fitted it.
status            Primary contact   (fitted, GPS fixes >= 5)
                  Limited-GPS contact (fitted, 1-4 GPS fixes)
                  GPS-free contact  (fitted, 0 GPS fixes)
                  Not fitted: <reason> (did not meet the 301-measurement rule
                  or had no accepted Doppler measurements).

FIT RESULT (batch least-squares corrections)
----------------------------------------------------------------
time_correction_s       Fitted along-track time correction (seconds) applied
                        to move the spacecraft along its initial orbit.
time_correction_std_s   1-sigma uncertainty of that time correction.
frequency_correction_hz Fitted constant radio-frequency bias (Hz) added to
                        the predicted Doppler.
batch_healthy           True if the fit passed acceptance (converged, both
                        corrections found, not stopped at a limit).

POSITION ERROR
----------------------------------------------------------------
prior_median_error_km     Median position error using the initial orbit
                          estimate with NO time correction.
corrected_median_error_km Median position error AFTER applying the fitted
                          time correction.

EQUATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Per-fix 3-D position error (GPS is the reference truth):

    d_i = || r_est(t_i) - r_gps(t_i) ||_2 / 1000        [km]

    r_est(t_i) : estimated ECEF/ITRF position at GPS time t_i (metres),
                 from the TLE propagated + (corrected case) along-track
                 phase shift of time_correction_s.
    r_gps(t_i) : raw NovAtel BESTXYZ GPS position at the same epoch (metres).
    ||.||_2    : Euclidean norm = sqrt(dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2).

Contact result (the reported number):

    corrected_median_error_km = median over i of d_i

    (prior_median_error_km is the same median but with the time correction
     forced to zero.)

A long or GPS-rich contact therefore cannot dominate: each contact
contributes one median. The report also uses RMS elsewhere (not in this CSV):

    RMS = sqrt( (1/N) * sum_i d_i^2 )
