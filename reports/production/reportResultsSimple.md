# Simplified pass results

The tables below list the 15 passes used for orbit fitting. Pass duration is the full recorded contact interval. Offset is the estimated TLE time offset; a positive value evaluates the prior TLE later in its orbit. Bias is the constant frequency correction estimated for the pass.

Prior and new TLE accuracy are the median three-dimensional position errors against GPS during the same pass. The new TLE accuracy applies the estimated offset to the prior TLE; the adjusted state can be expressed as a re-epoched TLE. Frequency bias is a receiver/model correction and is not encoded in the TLE.
