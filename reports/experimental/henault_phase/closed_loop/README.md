# Archived closed-loop simulation runs

These JSON files are deterministic single-geometry simulations of dither
acquisition and the experimental UKF/controller. They include both Doppler
and synthetic Doppler-plus-phase runs and vary delivery delay, jitter, or the
initial offset.

They are retained as implementation/regression artifacts. Their hidden truth,
exact baseline, and simulated observation stream mean that they do not
validate real acquisition, real antenna steering, or operational
phase-difference performance.
